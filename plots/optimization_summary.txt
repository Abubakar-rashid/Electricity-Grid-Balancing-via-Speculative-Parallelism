Optimization Comparison: Baseline vs Adaptive Task Granularity
========================================================================

OPTIMIZATION DETAILS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Baseline : Fixed chunk size (every chunk = chunkSize candidates)
Optimized: Dynamic chunk size  Formula: max(10, remaining / (workers*2))
Benefit  : Larger chunks early (fewer round-trips) -> smaller chunks
           late (finer load balance, fewer idle worker-cycles)
